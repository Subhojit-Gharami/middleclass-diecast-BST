COLLECT SMART

Simple rules for safer trades

01

Verify first

Ask for current photos/videos, packaging details and proof of ownership before paying or shipping.

02

Use your judgement

Middleclass Diecast BST is a community listing board, not a payment or escrow service. Deal directly at your own risk.

03

Keep it honest

Describe card, blister, box, paint and condition accurately. Do not list counterfeit or misleading items.

04

Meet safely

For local deals, choose a public place and follow your own safety precautions.
